How to use pauses, speed, pitch and emphasis (select text, then click a button above the text box)

<break time="500ms"/> or <break time="1.5s"/> = silent pause
<prosody rate="+20%" pitch="-5Hz" volume="+10%">text</prosody> = change a part only. Words also work: rate slow/fast/x-slow/x-fast, pitch low/high, volume soft/loud
<emphasis>text</emphasis> = slower and louder
Numbers: rate="-20%" slower, rate="+20%" faster; pitch="+10Hz" higher; volume="-20%" softer. Words: x-slow slow fast x-fast, low high, soft loud.
Combine in one tag: <prosody rate="slow" pitch="low" volume="soft">whisper-like line</prosody>
Tags add on top of the speaker's own settings. Example: A: This is <emphasis>really</emphasis> important.<break time="700ms"/> Listen carefully.

Speed: -50% is half speed, +100% is double. Small changes (-10% to +15%) sound most natural.
